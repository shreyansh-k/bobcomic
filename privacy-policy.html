Bob Comix Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Bob Comix is a browser new-tab extension. This policy explains what information the extension uses and how it is handled.

Information the extension uses

  • Approximate location: If you enable weather or air quality, the extension asks Edge for your location. Latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo to return weather and air-quality information. Location is not stored by the extension.
  • Preferences: Background, search-engine, widget, calendar-event, and shortcut settings are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to the developer.
  • Website shortcuts: When a shortcut is displayed, its website hostname is sent to CompanyEnrich to retrieve a logo. The shortcut destination itself is opened only when you choose it.
  • Festival information and fonts: The extension requests festival information from its configured festival service and loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Searches and external websites

Searches are sent directly to the search engine you select. Custom shortcuts and links open the websites you choose. Those websites and services have their own privacy policies, which apply to their processing.

Sharing and sale

Bob Comix does not sell personal information and does not maintain a developer-operated account or analytics database for this extension. Data sent to Open-Meteo, CompanyEnrich, the festival service, Google Fonts, search engines, and websites you open is handled by those providers under their respective policies.

Data retention and deletion

Local preferences remain in your browser until you remove them, uninstall the extension, or clear the extension's site data. You can disable location-based widgets by turning them off in Settings or denying Edge's location permission.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the extension's data practices change. For questions or support, visit the project owner's GitHub profile.
